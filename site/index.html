Questions

The things everyone asks

Is the mockup really free?

Yes. We build your new homepage first and show it to you. If you don't want it, you don't pay and we don't chase you. We'd rather show you the work than talk about it.

What do you need from me to start?

Your business name, your trade, and your current website if you have one. That's it. We can work from your existing site, your Google listing, and your social pages.

How long does it take?

The free mockup usually comes back within a few days. Once you approve it, a finished site typically goes live in about a week, depending on how much content we're waiting on.

What if I already have a domain?

We'll use it. It stays registered in your name, and we point it at the new site. If you don't have one yet, we'll help you get it — also in your name.

Can I update the site myself later?

Yes. We'll walk you through the handful of things owners actually change — hours, services, photos, phone number. If you'd rather not, the Care Plan covers it.

What happens to my old site?

Nothing until you say so. Your current site stays exactly as it is while we build. We only switch it over once you've seen the new one and given the go-ahead.

See your new site before you decide.

Takes thirty seconds to ask. There's no call, no deposit, and no obligation — just your business, rebuilt.
