Big heart

Every December, the bays fill up with dog food.

Hyde Park Auto and Just In Time Towing run an annual Christmas shelter pet food drive out of 4152 Albany Post Road. Customers send Chewy boxes straight to the shop or drop off food, treats, toys and cleaning supplies through December. It has gone to Hudson Valley Animal Rescue & Sanctuary and to Compassionate Animal Rescue Efforts of Dutchess County.

“Dear Maddie and friends from Hyde Park Auto and Jeff from Just in Time Towing, we are writing to express our heartfelt gratitude for your generous support of our shelter. Thanks to all of you, we were able to provide nutritious meals and fun activities for our furry friends during the holidays.”

“Your generosity made a huge difference in the lives of the animals that are still waiting to find their forever homes.”

Karen and the team at CARE of Dutchess County

They also make donations in memory of customers’ pets, and they will put a lost dog poster on the page before they put up an advert for themselves. “Small business. Big heart” is on their homepage. This is the receipt.
