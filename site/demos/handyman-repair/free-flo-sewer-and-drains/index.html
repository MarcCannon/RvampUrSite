What we do

Plumbing, drains and septic — start to finish.

Free Flo is a local plumbing and drainage company serving homeowners across the Hudson Valley, with commercial work by arrangement. Same crew from the phone call to the clean-up.

Drain cleaning

Clogged kitchen, bath or main line cleared — including descaling and snaking with the camera running so you can watch the blockage go.

Sewer camera inspections

We put a camera down the line and turn the screen toward you. Roots, bellies and breaks get found before anybody digs.

Septic repair & installation

From a failing baffle to a brand new leach field. We run our own excavator, so the dig, the install and the grading are one crew.

Drainage repair & replacement

Pipe repair, drain line replacement and re-routes — under the slab, in the crawlspace or out in the yard.

Well water & filtration

UV and sediment filters, softeners, pressure tanks and well pump service — the whole treatment wall, plumbed and labelled.

General plumbing

Water heaters, toilets, utility sinks, sump and sewage pumps, washer hook-ups. There is no project too small.
