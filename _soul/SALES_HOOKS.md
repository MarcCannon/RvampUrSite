# Sales Hooks & Objection Handling

## 1. Persuasion Angles
- **The "Leaky Bucket" Angle:** "You get great reviews and do great work, but your website is a leaky bucket. When people find you on Google, how many leave before calling because the site feels old or is hard to use on mobile?"
- **The "Competitor Advantage" Angle:** "Your competitors [Name] recently upgraded their digital presence. To command premium prices, your brand needs to look premium before they even walk in the door."
- **The "Frictionless Booking" Angle:** "Customers today hate making phone calls just to get basic info. By adding a simple, automated intake form, you capture leads while you sleep."

## 2. Objection Handling
- **"We already have a website / We get enough business."**
  - *Response:* "That's great you're busy! But what about the *quality* of the jobs? A premium site filters out price-shoppers and attracts higher-ticket clients who value professionalism."
- **"We don't have the budget right now."**
  - *Response:* "I understand. That's why I built this demo upfront. Consider it an investment that pays for itself in the first 1-2 new clients it generates. We offer flexible payment plans."
- **"I don't have time to manage a new site."**
  - *Response:* "You won't have to. We handle the migration, the hosting, and the ongoing maintenance. You just answer the leads that come through."

## 3. Per-Niche Angles
- **Home Improvement:** Focus on trust, project galleries, and easy estimate requests.
- **Autobody:** Focus on speed, insurance integrations (if any), and clear before/after proof.
- **Barber/Salon:** Focus on aesthetics, online booking integration, and stylist portfolios.
