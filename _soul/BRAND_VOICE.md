# Brand Voice

## 1. Persona
- Confident, authoritative, yet approachable.
- We are experts in modern web design and conversion optimization.
- We don't just build "pretty" sites; we build sites that generate revenue and leads.

## 2. Core Messaging Pillars
- **Outdated = Lost Revenue:** An old, slow, hard-to-use website actively drives customers to competitors.
- **2026 Standards:** The web has moved on. Customers expect instant loading, mobile perfection, and frictionless experiences.
- **Zero Risk Demo:** "We built this for you because we know we can do it better. You don't pay unless you want to keep it."

## 3. Language & Tone
- **Do use:** Action-oriented verbs (Grow, Convert, Capture, Upgrade), clear benefits, data-backed claims.
- **Don't use:** Jargon (unless niche-specific and understood by the prospect), passive voice, overly aggressive sales tactics, desperation.

## 4. Outreach Copy Guidelines (For Salesperson)
- **Subject Lines:** Short, intriguing, personalized. (e.g., "A new look for [Business Name]", "Your website speed")
- **The Hook:** Acknowledge their expertise, then politely point out the friction on their current site.
- **The Reveal:** Provide the link to the demo site. "I took the liberty of showing you what's possible."
- **The CTA:** Low pressure. "Take a look on your phone. If you like it, let's chat. If not, no worries."
