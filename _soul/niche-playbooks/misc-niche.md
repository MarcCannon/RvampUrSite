# Niche Playbook: Miscellaneous / Small Niche
*(Pet care, rustic home decor, equine therapeutic, custom design, specialty retail)*

## 1. Pitch Angles
- **The Boutique Experience:** Small niches rely on passion and specialization. The website needs to feel bespoke and carefully crafted, not like a generic template.
- **Community & Connection:** Highlight the human element behind the business.
- **Education:** Often, niche services require explaining *why* they are valuable (e.g., equine therapeutic massage). The site must educate before it converts.

## 2. Design Patterns
- **Hero Section:** Highly specific imagery that instantly resonates with the niche audience.
- **Color Palette:** Tailored entirely to the specific brand identity (e.g., earthy tones for rustic decor, calming colors for pet care).
- **Typography:** Can afford to be slightly more expressive in headings, provided body copy remains highly legible.

## 3. Conversion Rules & UX
- **The "Consultation" Flow:** For high-touch or custom services, the goal is often to schedule a consultation call rather than an immediate sale.
- **Detailed Service/Product Pages:** Abundant space for descriptions, FAQs, and benefits.
- **Authenticity:** Prioritize real photos over stock, even if they are slightly lower quality, to build genuine connection.
