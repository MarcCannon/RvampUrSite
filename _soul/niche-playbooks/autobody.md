# Niche Playbook: Autobody

## 1. Pitch Angles
- **Trust & Transparency:** Accidents are stressful. The website must immediately project reliability and professionalism.
- **Speed of Service:** Highlight fast turnaround times and streamlined processes.
- **Before/After Proof:** Visual evidence of repair quality is the highest converting asset.

## 2. Design Patterns
- **Hero Section:** High-quality image of a pristine repair facility or a finished car. Clear, urgent CTA like "Get a Free Estimate" or "Schedule Repairs".
- **Color Palette:** Often blues, silvers, and reds (conveying trust, technology, and action).
- **Navigation:** Simple. Services, Gallery, Reviews, Contact. Prominent "Call Now" button on mobile.

## 3. Conversion Rules & UX
- **Frictionless Estimate Form:** The primary conversion goal. Needs to be simple: Name, Phone, Vehicle Make/Model, Damage Description, and optionally photo upload.
- **Social Proof:** Prominently display Google Reviews and any industry certifications (I-CAR, ASE) near the CTA.
- **Mobile Priority:** Many users will be searching for an autobody shop on their phone immediately after an accident. The mobile experience must be lightning fast and easy to navigate with one hand.
