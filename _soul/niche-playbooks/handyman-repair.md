# Niche Playbook: Handyman & Repair

## 1. Pitch Angles
- **Trust & Safety:** People are inviting a stranger into their home. The site must immediately establish trustworthiness and background-checked reliability.
- **"No Job Too Small":** Clarify the scope. Make it easy for them to just ask a question.
- **Speed of Response:** "We answer the phone."

## 2. Design Patterns
- **Hero Section:** Friendly, approachable imagery. An actual photo of the owner/truck works better than generic stock photos of a hammer.
- **Color Palette:** Blue (trust), Orange (action/repair), White (cleanliness).
- **Service Grid:** A clear visual grid of exactly what they fix (plumbing, electrical, drywall, etc.).

## 3. Conversion Rules & UX
- **Frictionless "Get Help" Form:** Name, Phone, ZIP Code, and "What needs fixing?".
- **Review Prominence:** Highlight reviews that specifically mention punctuality, cleanliness, and fair pricing.
- **Service Area:** Explicitly state (and visually map) the neighborhoods/cities served to avoid unqualified leads.
