# Niche Playbook: Bakery & Restaurant

## 1. Pitch Angles
- **Sensory Appeal:** The website must make the user hungry. High-quality, appetizing photography is non-negotiable.
- **Convenience:** Easy access to menus, hours, location, and online ordering/reservations.
- **Atmosphere:** The digital experience should match the physical vibe of the establishment.

## 2. Design Patterns
- **Hero Section:** High-resolution, edge-to-edge photo or subtle video loop of signature dishes or the interior ambiance.
- **Color Palette:** Warm, inviting colors (creams, warm browns, deep reds) or clean, modern minimalism depending on the brand.
- **Menu Presentation:** MUST be text-based (HTML) for SEO and mobile readability, NEVER a PDF download.

## 3. Conversion Rules & UX
- **Prominent CTAs:** "Order Online", "Book a Table", "View Menu". These should be sticky on mobile.
- **Local SEO Focus:** Clear address, embedded Google Map, and easily clickable phone number.
- **Integration:** Seamless handoff to third-party ordering (Toast, Square, etc.) or reservation systems (Resy, OpenTable).
