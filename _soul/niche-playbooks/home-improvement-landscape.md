# Niche Playbook: Home Improvement & Landscape

## 1. Pitch Angles
- **Lifestyle Upgrade:** Sell the feeling of a beautiful home or yard, not just the service.
- **Reliability:** Address the common fear of flaky contractors. Emphasize communication, punctuality, and guarantees.
- **Project Scope:** Clearly define what you do and don't do to qualify leads effectively.

## 2. Design Patterns
- **Hero Section:** Stunning, large-scale imagery of completed projects (lush lawns, remodeled kitchens). Aspiration is key.
- **Color Palette:** Earth tones (greens, browns) for landscaping; clean, modern neutrals (whites, grays, navy) for home remodeling.
- **Portfolio/Gallery:** Crucial component. Needs to be easily filterable by project type.

## 3. Conversion Rules & UX
- **The "Request a Quote" Flow:** Needs to be prominent. Ask for project type, timeline, and basic contact info.
- **Before & Afters:** Interactive sliders or side-by-side images work exceptionally well here.
- **Trust Badges:** Display licenses, insurance info, and association memberships clearly in the footer and near forms.
