# Niche Playbook: Barber & Salon

## 1. Pitch Angles
- **The Modern Standard:** High-end clients expect a high-end booking experience. An old site devalues the perceived quality of the haircut/service.
- **Stylist Showcases:** Salons are driven by individual personalities. The site needs to sell the stylists, not just the salon name.
- **Frictionless Booking:** The easier it is to book, the fuller the chairs.

## 2. Design Patterns
- **Hero Section:** High-fashion, edgy, or ultra-clean photography depending on the specific salon's vibe.
- **Color Palette:** Often high contrast. Black and white for modern barbershops; muted, elegant pastels for high-end salons.
- **Service Menu:** Needs to be clear and categorized.

## 3. Conversion Rules & UX
- **"Book Now" Prominence:** This is the most critical button. It should be sticky and highly visible.
- **Direct Integration:** Must clearly hand off to their booking software (Mindbody, Vagaro, Phorest, Squire).
- **Instagram Feed:** A live feed or highly curated gallery of recent cuts/styles is essential social proof.
