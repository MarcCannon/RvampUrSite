# Niche Playbook: Smoke Shop

## 1. Pitch Angles
- **Compliance & Credibility:** A professional site signals a legitimate business, separating them from seedy competitors.
- **Inventory Discovery:** Customers want to know if you carry their specific brand of disposable, glass, or cigar before they drive over.
- **Local SEO Dominance:** Most traffic is "smoke shop near me". The site must be optimized to capture that intent.

## 2. Design Patterns
- **Age Gate (Mandatory):** A sleek, unobtrusive (but compliant) 21+ verification modal on the first visit.
- **Hero Section:** Focus on product variety and atmosphere. Neon accents often work well.
- **Color Palette:** Dark modes with vibrant accent colors (green, purple, neon blue).

## 3. Conversion Rules & UX
- **The "Click and Collect" Pattern:** Even if they don't sell online (due to payment processor restrictions), a browseable catalog that allows users to "reserve in store" drives massive foot traffic.
- **Catalog Schema:** Inject Product schema markup for popular items to capture long-tail local searches.
- **Clear Store Info:** Hours, location, and phone number must be instantly accessible on mobile.
